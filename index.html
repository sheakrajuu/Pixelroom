Frequently asked questions

What can I do with Pixelroomedit?

Apply 16 filters, adjust brightness, contrast, saturation, warmth, highlights, shadows, fade, and vignette, remove distractions, crop, rotate, add a persistent PNG logo, compare changes, and export PNG, JPEG, or WebP files.

How does the editor work?

Select a photo, choose a filter or Edit adjustment, then paint over what should disappear or use Auto-detect text. Review the red mask, choose Remove selection, add branding if needed, and preview the final export before downloading.

Is my photo uploaded anywhere?

Photos, masks, filters, adjustments, and branding placement are processed locally in this browser. The OCR library and optional ONNX runtime are loaded from a CDN, but your image is not uploaded. Remove metadata can strip EXIF details such as GPS data from the export.
